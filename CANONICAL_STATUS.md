# Canonical status of the Epistemic Octahedron

## Frozen canonical discovery

The frozen canonical statement of the Epistemic Octahedron is the paper **Epistemic Octahedron: The Objective Model of Philosophical Maturity**, identified by DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22164942`.

The canonical theory is not a rolling community-edited framework. Repository documentation may be corrected to match the paper, but preference, popularity, institutional adoption, credentials, terminology, or a downstream instrument do not revise the discovery.

The older Zenodo records and the older material retained under `paper/` remain part of the publication history. They do not override the frozen canonical paper.

## What remains open

Anyone may study, cite, teach, test, criticize, compare, or attempt to refute the Epistemic Octahedron. Independent downstream instruments, software, visualizers, datasets, interview methods, scoring systems, and validation studies may also be developed, subject to the paper's rights notice and the repository's instrumentation policy.

Those activities do not automatically alter the canonical EO.

## Reciprocal structural challenge

A challenge to the discovery must engage the structure actually claimed by the canonical paper. A substantive refutation may establish one or more of the following:

1. an internal contradiction;
2. a failure in the derivation of a necessary function;
3. a formed worldview that escapes the five-function structure;
4. an additional necessary and irreducible function of formed worldview reasoning;
5. failure of the objective-dominance relation;
6. a correspondence failure in which the geometry cannot preserve a maturity-relevant distinction required by the fixed semantics.

The Five-Function Invariance Test gives a direct closure challenge: if a genuine referent-grounded philosophical superiority survives while concern, constraint, information, interpretation, and epistemic stability are all genuinely held fixed, the exhaustion claim is false and the structural field requires revision.

A challenge to a downstream instrument has a different burden. Poor reliability, invalid operationalization, biased sampling, unstable scoring, weak prediction, or bad calibration may defeat an instrument without defeating the underlying structural discovery.

## Canonical correction procedure

Because the main paper is frozen, it should not be silently rewritten. If a genuine substantive defect is ever demonstrated, the correction should be documented publicly and issued as a distinct publication record while preserving the existing DOI-bearing paper as provenance.

Repository reader references may be corrected whenever they drift from the frozen paper. Such documentation corrections do not alter the theory.

## Derived and competing models

A modified, expanded, or competing framework should identify itself as independent, derived, modified, or competing. Retaining the EO name, using an octahedron, or claiming an improvement does not by itself make another framework canonical.

The canonical standard remains the structure stated in DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22164942` unless a substantive defect is actually demonstrated and publicly corrected.
