# Defensive publication statement

## Public disclosure and priority

The Epistemic Octahedron was publicly disclosed in Version 1.0 on July 29, 2026, under DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21688197`.

The DOI-bearing publication, repository copy, source, figures, checksums, and preserved version history are intended to establish a permanent public record of the discovery, its author, its disclosed structure, and its publication date.

## Anti-enclosure intention

Raul Miguel Pascual Paras III does not seek exclusive patent control over the EO core disclosed in the Version 1.0 publication.

The public disclosure is intended to function as a defensive publication against later claims that another person or organization independently originated, exclusively owns, or may privately enclose the same disclosed geometry, semantic structure, definitions, propositions, or instrument-design requirements.

The disclosed EO core should remain publicly available for study, criticism, testing, teaching, and independently authored downstream implementation.

## Limits of this statement

This statement records the author's intention and the purpose of the public disclosure. It is not a legal opinion, a patent search, a guarantee that every later patent application will be refused, or a determination of rights in every jurisdiction.

Public disclosure may be relevant prior art against later patent claims, but novelty, inventiveness, claim construction, subject-matter eligibility, filing dates, and jurisdiction-specific law remain matters for the relevant patent office or court.

This statement does not grant rights in third-party patents, trademarks, copyrighted works, confidential information, or other property. It also does not alter the paper rights stated in `LICENSE-PAPER.txt`.

A downstream instrument developer may create genuinely new software, measurement procedures, datasets, interfaces, or validation methods and may hold rights in those additions. Such rights do not transfer authorship of the EO discovery, alter the canonical theory, erase the original priority record, or create exclusive ownership of the EO core already disclosed to the public.
