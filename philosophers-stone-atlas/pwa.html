The Philosopher's Stone

This page installs and maintains an offline copy of the Stone Atlas on this iPhone.

Preparing offline copy…
  1. Keep this page open until it says the offline copy is ready.
  2. In Safari, tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Launch Stone from the new Home Screen icon.

After setup, cached Stone Atlas files are served from the iPhone. Use the update button whenever you want to refresh the local copy from GitHub Pages.
