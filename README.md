## Structural discovery

The Epistemic Octahedron identifies a closed structural field of formed worldview reasoning. A formed worldview is a completed structured stance toward reality. Its philosophically relevant orientation is exhausted by five necessary functions:

- empathy: concern for affected welfare;
- practicality: real-world constraint;
- knowledge: informational grounding;
- wisdom: contextual interpretation and discernment;
- epistemic stability: the reality-governed condition of the organized whole under materially relevant pressure.

These five functions resolve into three signed structural relations:

```text
E <-> P : purposive relation
K <-> W : meaning-forming relation
S       : answerability to reality under pressure
```

Concern and constraint organize what judgment is for in relation to welfare and executable reality. Information and interpretation organize what represented reality means in context. Epistemic stability states whether that organized purpose and meaning remain governable by evidence, consequence, constraint, affected welfare, contradiction, and correction.

The paper's foundational proof architecture is:

1. necessary worldview relations;
2. structural closure;
3. objective dominance by non-loss plus correction;
4. the unique integrated maximum `(0,1,0)`.

Closure is falsifiable. If a genuine philosophical superiority can survive complete invariance of concern, constraint, information, interpretation, and epistemic stability, then the five-function exhaustion claim is false and the structure requires revision.

## Geometry

Let

```math
\mathbb O:=\{p\in\mathbb R^3:\lVert p\rVert_1\le 1\},
\qquad
\partial\mathbb O:=\{p\in\mathbb R^3:\lVert p\rVert_1=1\}.
```

Completed surface-eligible referents satisfy

```math
|x|+|y|+|z|=1.
```

The surface equation is a completion constraint. No radial or ray projection is part of the EO semantics. An arbitrary interior triple is not normalized outward into a worldview coordinate.

For a surface-eligible referent `r`, the complete semantic coordinate is

```math
\sigma(r)=\left(\Delta_r(E,P),\Sigma_r,\Delta_r(W,K)\right),
```

with

```math
|\Delta_r(E,P)|+|\Sigma_r|+|\Delta_r(W,K)|=1.
```

The directional semantics are fixed:

```text
+x = Empathy                 -x = Practicality
+z = Wisdom                  -z = Knowledge
+y = positive stability      -y = negative stability
```

The distinguished states are:

```text
O = (0, 0, 0)   pre-philosophical null
M = (0, 1, 0)   objective philosophical maturity
C = (0,-1, 0)   epistemic collapse
```

On the surface, unresolved lateral displacement is `|x|+|z|=1-|y|`. It is maximal at the equator and vanishes at both vertical limits. The upper limit resolves displacement through reality-governed integration. The lower limit resolves it through correction-resistant insulation from reality.

## Interior, evidence, and referents

The interior does not contain completed worldview coordinates. Genuine orientation underdetermination during formation or re-formation may be represented by an auxiliary interior orientation without being treated as a completed worldview point.

Evaluator uncertainty is a different problem. If a formed worldview or bounded referent has a determinate surface location but the available evidence does not identify it exactly, the faithful report is an evidence-warranted region `\Omega(r\mid e)` on the eligible surface. Exact coordinates require a singleton warranted region.

EO plots must declare their referent. Whole worldviews and plot-eligible bounded referents such as convictions, states, reasoning fragments, texts, institutions, procedures, algorithms, or systems use the same surface semantics while remaining distinct referent classes. A narrow local plot does not certify an entire person or worldview.

## Consequences and extensions

The canonical paper develops the structural discovery into truth-relation and accountability, epistemic freedom, moral objectivity, source-mediated learning, source weight and proportional verification, unequal-contribution comparison in shared inquiry, maturity-proxy substitution, institutional judgment and procedural bloat, comparison with leading wisdom research, applications, objections, and explicit falsification conditions.
